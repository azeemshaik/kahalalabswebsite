The Problem

The US Fiber Supply Chain Is Broken

AI data center buildouts are consuming fiber at 36x the rate of traditional CPU racks. The two dominant US suppliers are locked into exclusive contracts, and the remaining import channels carry prohibitive tariffs or lead-time risk.

Incumbents Locked

The two dominant US fiber manufacturers have committed $12B+ in exclusive multi-year contracts with major hyperscalers. Neither is taking new customers at competitive terms.

12+ month lead times

Tariff & Compliance Wall

Section 301, AD/CVD, and country-of-origin tariffs on key import channels exceed 104%. BEAD Buy America requirements further narrow the field of compliant suppliers.

104%+ combined tariffs

AI Demand Explosion

A single NVL72 GPU rack requires 1,152 fibers at 800G. AI racks consume 36x more fiber than traditional CPU racks. Every new cluster means thousands of kilometers of cable.

36x more fiber per rack

The result: data center operators, AI neoclouds, and telecoms are stuck between locked incumbents, tariff-heavy imports, and exploding demand. Kahala Labs sources the most cost-effective, schedule-reliable supply chain for each customer.
